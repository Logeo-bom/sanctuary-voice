Engineering Methodology

Specification-Driven AI vs. "Vibe Coding"

As educator Dave Farley highlighted in "The Industry Is Catching Up: The Only Way to Generate AI Code That Works", programming in natural language does not mean typing loose prompts and expecting production results.

❌ Vague Prompting ("Vibe Coding")

"Write a mobile church translator app using AI speech."

  • Builds a generic near-field translator (assumes phone 2 inches from mouth).
  • OS noise cancellation destroys distant loudspeaker audio.
  • Bilingual stage speech collides into scrambled captions.
  • Organ synth pads cause endless audio lockup.
✅ Dave Farley Spec-Driven Architecture

"Define sanctuary acoustic physics & test edge cases first."

  • Spec 1: +22dB back-row pre-amp with 6:1 compression.
  • Spec 2: 90Hz low-cut + 280Hz worship synth notch filter.
  • Spec 3: Stage diarization with 1-tap interpreter muting.
  • Spec 4: Deepgram streaming WebSocket + Gemini Flash.
Acoustic & Hardware Architecture

The 4 Sanctuary Physics Specs

1. Weak PA Loudspeaker & Back Pews

Bypass Android OS call gating (echoCancellation: false). Implement a 3-tier pre-amp (+2dB, +12dB Pew Boost, +22dB Back Row) with a 6:1 compressor so whispering and loud shouts stay balanced.

2. Worship Synth Pad Frequency Ducking

Continuous organ/pad chords fool standard voice detection into never pausing. We applied a 90Hz high-pass (room rumble), a 280Hz notch (pad mud), and a 2.4kHz vocal presence boost.

3. Stage Interpreter & Congregational Shouting

Solves Bard's Brazilian church experience: Pastor preaches on PA while a lady shouts/prays in tongues on the floor. Deepgram tags speakers; user has a 1-tap voice lock to mute all ambient shouting in their earphones.

4. Theological & Regional Dialects

Theological concepts (justification, grace, sanctification) and local vernacular (Swiss French huitante, Brazilian Portuguese) are preserved via injected theological context prompts.

Prompt Engineering & Guardrails

The Actual Prompts & Specifications Applied

1. AI System Prompt (English Specification for LLM & STT)

Role: "You are SanctuaryVoice, an expert theological and multilingual church stage interpreter."

Input: Raw streaming audio captured inside a reverberant church auditorium.

Specifications & Rules:

  • Speaker Separation: Diarize and distinguish between Speaker 1 (Pulpit Pastor), Translator 2 (Stage Interpreter), and ambient congregation voices.
  • Instrument Filtering: Duck or ignore background worship music (organ chords, keyboard synth pads).
  • Spiritual Tongues / Shouting: If an audience member shouts or prays in tongues, tag as [Congregation Vocalization] and keep the pastor's sermon clear.
  • Natural Delivery: Translate into dignified, reverent language suitable for personal earphone listening.
2. Audio Hardware & Filter Pipeline (How the Mic Pre-Cleans Sound)
Step 1: Raw Mic Access Turn off phone noise cancellation so distant speakers aren't muted.
Step 2: 90Hz Low-Cut Filter Strips out HVAC air conditioning hum and stage floor vibrations.
Step 3: 280Hz Synth Notch Filter Cuts the warm muddy frequencies where church organ pads resonate.
Step 4: +22dB Pre-Amp & Compressor Amplifies weak back-pew volume while compressing loud shouts so audio never clips.
